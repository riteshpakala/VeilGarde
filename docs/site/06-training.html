How is each shipped model trained?

On the training regime: every model the detector ships, trained from data in Swift, and measured against the one it would replace.

The shipped models were trained in Python, across scripts that were not all committed. VeilGarde trains every one of them in Swift, from the same data, with the same recipes, and writes a bundle the detector runs as it is.

Each trained model is measured beside the shipped one on the same rows. Where the shipped model saw those rows in training, the measurement says so.

A run is one declaration

A training run is one recipe, written the way a SwiftUI body is: one model per line, with an if or a for where the set depends on something. Each model is a value with copy modifiers, so nothing is built until the engine runs it.

The engine has two calls. The plan reads files and touches no GPU: it lists each model's stages, whether each runs and why, and any refusal. Training plans first, so a refusal stops the run before a candidate exists.

A candidate starts as a copy of its parent bundle. Each trained model replaces its parent's file, every write re-hashes the manifest, and the whole candidate is verified at the end. veilgarde route --bundle runs it.

Trees without XGBoost

Three of the shipped models are tree ensembles, and there is no XGBoost in Swift. VeilGarde's trainer reproduces XGBoost 3.2's histogram method decision for decision. Gradients and gains are single precision and histograms double, each sibling's histogram is built by subtraction, and ties break toward the lower feature, then the lower bin.

On integer features its trees equal XGBoost's node for node, in three configurations. On continuous features the bins differ slightly, and the AUC stays within 0.005. A warm start copies the parent's trees into the new file verbatim, so they stay byte-identical.

The warp tile model

The lens cut final_mixed's 12,000 images into 2,511,615 tiles of 19 features, once, in 27 minutes. One image does not decode and is skipped.

The trainer then fits optimal_8's shape: 200 trees of depth 8 at a learning rate of 0.1, with four fifths of the rows and of the features sampled per tree. On the 1,828,099 training tiles it took 26 seconds in release.

The calibration

The camera-sensor score's contract is fitted the way the shipped one was. The fraction of tiles above 0.5 is Platt-scaled on its logit over 65% of the validation images. The threshold is chosen on the other 35%: the highest recall whose real false-positive rate stays at or under 0.10. The engine then says the fitted contract as the two cuts on the vote where its call changes, and writes those: the detector reads the cuts.

That rule is the reference choose_operating_threshold, ported exactly. On the model trained here it gave a coefficient of 0.9921, an intercept of −0.0577 and a threshold of 0.6448, at a recall of 0.517.

The fusion head

The base grows by a warm start, the flywheel's only way to move it. On the seed store's 113,176 rows, 80 trees at a learning rate of 0.03 took 38 seconds and kept the parent's 940 trees byte for byte. Recall on the held-out new-generator rows rose from 0.9955 to 0.9975.

The heads

Each head is a standardized logistic regression, fitted by Newton's method to sklearn's own objective. It reaches sklearn's optimum within 1e-5. Each kind keeps its shipped recipe: which rows are positive, which negative, the regularization, the class weights, and a source-disjoint test split.

The combiner

The combiner is refitted under its constraint that every weight stays non-negative, on 97,166 capture-absent store rows. Its rescue weight is chosen by a sweep that must keep every protect-real probe real, and the sweep picks the shipped 0.5. Pooled, the test AUC rose from 0.9896 to 0.9901 and the real false-positive rate fell from 3.1% to 1.2%.

It is a new fit, not a reproduction. The shipped weights were fitted on another cache under a different fusion model.

The noiseprint

The noiseprint learns from camera originals against everything that is not a direct capture: AI photographs, AI art, web reals, screenshots, AI-video frames and photoreal forgeries. Web reals matter most. They began in a camera and were laundered, and a net that cannot beat them is not reading a camera.

Every image is recompressed at one of five levels drawn at random, in both classes alike, so compression cannot become the label. The net trains for six epochs, and after every step its first layer is projected back onto the constraint that makes it read residuals. Its BatchNorms are then folded into the four convolutions the detector runs.

On 11,948 patches the six epochs took 19 seconds. Tested on 88 held-out camera originals against 30 images of each family, the net reached an AUC of 0.955, against the shipped net's 0.942 on the same images. Recompression barely moved it: 0.956 at JPEG 0.75, 0.950 at HEIC.

That first retrain could not replace the shipped net. One image is a mandatory check: a photoreal AI picture that forged a capture peak of 568, which a noiseprint must not read as a camera. The first retrain read it at 0.578, the shipped net at 0.429. In the combiner a high camera reading subtracts generated evidence, so the run said what the next training set needed: photoreal forgeries among the negatives.

The second retrain added them: 115 full-resolution photoreal AI images from Instagram, the anchor left out of every folder by path and by content. Three rules were fixed before it ran. The anchor reads under 0.5; the pooled AUC stays within 0.005 of the shipped net's; the photoreal family's AUC is at least the shipped net's. It met all three: the anchor at 0.386, the pooled AUC 0.963 against 0.947, the photoreal family 0.989 against 0.978.

With it the full battery still passes, and no call changes. The net is staged as a candidate. Replacing the shipped one is the owner's decision.

The backbones

DINOv2 is not trained here; it is imported. The import starts from Hugging Face's checkpoint, renames each tensor, moves the patch convolution's axes, and resizes the 37×37 position grid to 16×16 for a 224-pixel input.

That resize has to be PyTorch's, bit for bit, or the file would differ from the shipped export. Plain single-precision arithmetic matched 44% of the values. Fusing the multiply-adds the compiler fused in PyTorch's kernel, in the source position, the weights and the sums, matches every value at every scale tried.

The imported file is byte-identical to the shipped one, so its hash is the manifest's pin. The F16 and 8-bit variants match Garde's tensor for tensor.

For engineers: where each piece lives
  • Training's folders run in order: Data, Extract, Boost and Linear, then one folder per model, then Recipe, Engine and Artifacts.
  • veilgarde train plan --preset <datasets>/final_mixed prints the shipped recipe's plan without training.
  • The tests hold the trainers to references written once in Python: XGBoost's trees, scikit-learn's logistic fits and scipy's bounded optimum.
