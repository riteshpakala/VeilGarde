How does the flywheel grow the detector?

On the regime that keeps the stack current as generators change, without ever keeping an image.

Generators change every month, and a detector trained once falls behind. The answer is a flywheel. Find where the camera head is wrong, fetch images of that kind, and train candidates on them. Promote only what passes a battery of images the detector must never get wrong.

The flywheel moves the camera head, the combiner and the linear heads, and nothing else. A new member of the stack joins the loop by joining the recipe. It stores the features those models read, never the images they came from.

A store of features

Every image becomes one row of three columns: DINOv2's embedding after the heads' forced resize, DINOv2's embedding of the plain crop the camera head reads, and the nine capture values. That is everything a head, the camera head or the combiner trains on. The image is read, measured in memory and dropped.

The shard format is VeilGarde's version 1, written in each shard's header, and a shard of any other format is refused. On 2026-10-21 veilgarde store migrate rewrote the store's 60 shards, 138,267 rows, into that format with the three columns alone. Every shard's rows and each column's bytes were checked equal after the write. The warp score, the sharpness and the noiseprint the research's shards also held were left behind in the old folder, which was moved aside and never written.

VeilGarde's own rows add provenance: the source and its URL, the licence, the native size, a difference hash and the CLIP router's scores.

Aiming at the blind spots

The gap miner reads the store and scores the live models on it, bucket by bucket: by domain, camera or processed, by category and by label. A bucket's gap is how often the camera head is wrong plus how often it disagrees with the pooled verdict.

On the July cache, under the warp-era head, it found the research's two worst buckets in the research's order: processed generated photographs, then camera-domain ones. Generated photographs were the blind spot then. On 2026-10-21 the census found the head without the warp misses more of them.

Fetching without keeping

Sixteen sources answer the miner's jobs, from Wikimedia and the Met to Civitai and Bluesky's firehose, each with the Python collectors' retry rules and a cursor that resumes. Every fetched image passes a gate before it is measured. It must decode, measure at least 512 pixels on its short side and weigh at least 20 KB. It must also be new: not the same bytes as a stored row, and not a near copy by its difference hash.

Generated images are then routed by CLIP, the argmax of their embedding against a fixed matrix of category prompts. A row lands in the shard, and the bytes are gone.

On its first run it kept 20 of 29 fetched images as rows, refused the rest as already stored, and wrote no image.

Weekly candidates

Once a week the flywheel trains on top of live. The camera head is warm-started with 80 trees at a learning rate of 0.03, and the art, blur and human heads are trained from the whole store. Each candidate is a complete bundle, measured beside live, with a review file that says which slots to promote and why.

The measure is agreement: how often the camera head and the pooled verdict agree on the store's rows. On the live bundle seeded when the warp left, it reads 0.9702, with the head scored on rows it was trained on.

The battery

The battery is a set of images grouped by what the detector must do with them. Six groups are hard: every image in them must get the call its group wants. Two more are ratchets that must not fall below their recorded floors.

The owner named the shipped detector v1 on 2026-10-21 and made its battery the baseline for testing. The battery's spec, battery_spec.json, version 1, sets aside the four generated images v1 reads as real, in the spec's staged list for research. On every image left, v1 passes every group, and the ratchets' floors were recorded from that run.

Promote is the only writer of live

Promote takes one slot from a candidate into live: camera, combiner or a head. It refuses a frozen slot outright, and since 2026-10-21 the frozen slots are the weights alone. It refuses a camera head that does not keep live's trees byte for byte, a combiner with a negative weight or a member live has no head for, and a category head that fails admission.

Then it snapshots live, writes the slot, re-pins the manifest and runs the battery: the hard groups for the camera head, every group for anything else. On a failure it restores the snapshot.

It earned its place on its first day, under the warp-era head. That week's warm start flipped IMG_0291, a knife-edge image the battery holds, from generated to real, and promote refused it and restored live. A smaller warm start kept the image generated and promoted.

What is still open

The four images set aside wait for a candidate that calls them right; until one does, they are measured and not gated. What the detection lane tries next is the owner's question.

The atlas groups are retired, not replaced. Until a new atlas is built, the art, blur and human heads are gated by the eight groups that remain and by admission.

The warp era's live bundle, its candidates and its tile cache stay in the work area, moved aside and not deleted, as do the live bundle and the store from before the restructure. Whether to keep them is also the owner's question.

  • The work area's layout is in FlywheelArea; every stage but promote and rollback opens live read-only, and a test checks it.
  • veilgarde flywheel gap, acquire, ingest, candidates, agreement, battery, promote, rollback and status are the loop, one command each.
  • flywheel battery --gate full --rebaseline --yes-i-reviewed re-records the ratchets' floors; nothing else writes them.
