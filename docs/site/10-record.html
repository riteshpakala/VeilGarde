What did the research find?

On what VeilGarde measured before it became two lanes, the trainers that left with the warp, and the cut itself.

The detector that ships is smaller than the one VeilGarde was ported as. Each part that left was measured against the detector without it, by rules fixed before the numbers came in. This chapter is that record, kept on main after the code it describes moved to a branch.

Every number names its ledger row and its date. A number VeilGarde did not measure is marked as quoted.

The branch that keeps the rest

The branch 2026-rnd was cut from commit 6ed634d on 2026-10-21, before any of the cut's changes. It holds the detector as the studies measured it, and every study's code.

  • The warp lens and its 19 features per tile, the warp tile model, its sensor copy and the camera-sensor call's two cuts.
  • The noiseprint: its lens, its weights, its trainer and its store column.
  • The frozen fusion reference, zoom escalation, the render taxonomy with its arbiter and the cgi head, and the x-domain gate with its centroids.
  • The research module: the pruning study, the core study, the redundancy check, the condensed model and the detector matrix, with their commands and tests.
  • This site as it stood, with eleven code trails and seven ML trails.

To read it, check the branch out and open its site. The work area keeps that era's data beside the new: the old live bundle as live-warp-20261021, the warp-era candidates and the tile cache. The binary on main cannot read their manifests.

The pruning study, 2026-10-19

The first study took each part out and asked what the detector lost. A part was within tolerance when taking it out cost at most 0.005 AUC on held-out data and moved at most 0.5% of the calls.

Each difference carried a 95% interval from 1,000 resamples, so a difference too small to measure said so.

One control was added after the first run, under the same rules. A model refit with only its seed changed already moves some calls, so two re-seeds gave the noise floor every other refit is read against.

The cut to two lanes, measured

On 2026-10-21 the owner made detection the fusion head and the specialist stack, and gave the warp to the watermark alone. The owner set no bar: a drop was acceptable if its margin was negligible, and the flywheel would bring it back.

The fusion head was trained here from scratch on DINOv2 and capture, and the combiner refit on five inputs. Both shipped, the battery was rebaselined, and every number below is what was measured.
