What does the warp residual measure?

On the one physical measurement VeilGarde keeps: what a tile loses when it is warped a hair out of place and back.

A camera leaves a trace in its pixels that a generator does not. The sensor sees the scene through a mosaic of red, green and blue filters, and every pixel carries a little noise of its own. Warp a patch of those pixels a hair out of place and back, and the mosaic and the noise do not come back as they were.

A generator's output is smoother, and it survives the round trip differently. What the round trip loses is the warp residual. VeilGarde reads one number from it for every tile, the fragility rate, and since 2026-10-21 only the watermark reads it.

Thirty-two pixels at native size

The residual is read tile by tile, each tile a square of 32 by 32 pixels. The image is decoded once at its own size, with no cap and no padding, and only full tiles count.

Native size is the point. A crop must land on the same pixels in the same tiles as its source, so the matcher can find it. A resize would move every pixel to a new tile, and the pattern with it.

Sixteen warps, two strengths

Each tile is warped through 16 fixed homographies. Here a homography moves the tile's four corners and drags every pixel with them. The 16 corner moves were drawn once from a seeded generator and are kept as a literal table: every signature ever written was read through these warps and no others.

Each warp runs at two strengths, 0.025 and 0.05. At the stronger one a corner moves up to 1.6 pixels along each axis.

The tile goes forward and comes back through cv2's bicubic kernel and its 1/32-pixel coordinates. The way back keeps the reference's inverse of the inverse in float64 rather than the original matrix, because the two round differently and the signatures were read the first way.

The residual is the difference between each pixel and its value after the round trip, averaged over the 16 warps and the three colour channels. Only its high frequencies are kept: a 9×9 Gaussian blur at σ 1.5 is subtracted, the one parameter that won the research's v29 ablation.

One number a tile

The fragility rate compares the two strengths. It is the mean residual at 0.05, less the mean at 0.025, per unit of strength. A tile whose texture breaks up faster under the stronger warp has the higher rate.

The plane is cut into tiles from a chosen origin, so the same image can be read on a grid shifted by any number of pixels. Signing reads it from the top-left corner. Matching reads a crop from 16 origins, every 8 pixels in each direction, as chapter 8 tells.

The tiles go to the GPU in batches of 256, both strengths for each batch, and the rates come back in row-major order.

The rates are pinned. For the two parity crops, the tests hold every tile's rate and the signature's id exactly. A separate gate signs the battery's 105 images again and compares each signature with the recorded one.

What detection may read

The same residual also yields 19 features a tile at a 512-pixel cap, the warp19 the research's tile model read. Detection may take each feature's mean and spread over the image, 38 numbers, as fusion columns. The code that measures them lives here, beside the signature's, and detection reads only the numbers.

The signature never reads them. It reads FR alone at native size, and its pinned fragility rates and the invariance run did not move when warp19 came back.

Where the residual came from

Until 2026-10-21 the same residual fed the detector, and the branch 2026-rnd keeps that code.

Each tile became a row of 19 features. A tree model scored each tile, and the tiles gave a weighted verdict and a camera-sensor call. The record chapter keeps that model's numbers.

The research measured that the detector no longer needed it. Refit without the warp score, the fusion head lost 0.0012 AUC on held-out store rows, at the noise floor of a re-seed. The owner then gave the warp to the watermark alone.

The move changed no operation and no order. The residual's five files moved from the lens into the watermark. The tests held the pinned rates and ids bit for bit before the change, after detection lost the warp, and after the move.
