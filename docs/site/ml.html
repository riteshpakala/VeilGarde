The ML explorer

On how each shipped model learns and decides, and how the models together reach the two verdicts.

Every shipped model is told here as machine learning: what it learns from, its features, the algorithm and why, the fit, the calibration, and its part in the verdict. Six trails follow one model each, and a seventh follows the two verdicts, majority and weighted, from the fork between them to the evidence that each part is needed. Each step opens with a primer for a newcomer, then the note on the code, then a deeper line with the trade-off and the measured numbers. Every number is read from the shipped bundle or names its source, and each step links to its twin on the change explorer. The depth control chooses which of the three layers show, and what the marks mean is one click away, under the trail grid.
