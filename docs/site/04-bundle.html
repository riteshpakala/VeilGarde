What does the detector ship?

On the files the detector reads, how each is held to its hash, and which of them may never move.

The detector ships as a bundle: a folder of model files and one manifest that names them. It lives in Sources/VeilGardeInference/Resources and travels inside the package, so every build of VeilGarde carries the exact models it runs.

The bundle holds 8 artifacts beside its manifest. Two were trained here: the camera head and the stack combiner. Six are linear heads: screenshot, art, blur, human, semtile and preview.

The neural weights are not in it. The manifest records the 3 DINOv2 files it expects beside the bundle, and their hashes. The detector finds them in the work area or in a folder it is told about.

Every file held to its hash

Each artifact in the manifest carries its role, its file name, its SHA-256, its size and where its bytes came from. Loading a bundle re-hashes every file the manifest names and refuses a mismatch, as an error and never as a warning. A file the manifest does not name is ignored.

The shipped bundle is verified once per process, the first time anything reads it. Everything that reads a shipped model asks the bundle for it by role, so a file's name is free and its bytes are not.

The six heads are the reference's bytes, ported on 2026-10-19 and unchanged since. The camera head and the combiner were trained here, on the features-only store, and the manifest records the candidate each came from.

What may never move

A role is frozen when the flywheel may never promote over it. Since the warp left detection, the frozen roles are the weights alone: DINOv2's three variants and CLIP's vision tower.

The reason is what was fitted against those bytes. Every head and the camera head read a DINOv2 embedding, and a moved backbone would move every one of them at once. The camera head, the combiner and the heads are the models the flywheel exists to improve, so none of them is frozen.

Its history

Every seed, promote and rollback adds a line here. The history started again with v1 on 2026-10-21: one seed, the stack as a list of members. The earlier history, from the port to the two lanes, stays in the git history and in chapter 10.

The two files trained here

The camera head and the combiner were refit the day the warp left detection. The head before them also read the warp score and sharpness. A warm start cannot continue a head on columns it never saw, so the new one was grown from scratch.

Seven artifacts left the bundle the same day. They were the warp tile model and its sensor copy, the sensor calibration, the fusion reference, the cross-domain centroids, the cgi head and the noiseprint. Nothing in detection reads them any more. They stay on the 2026-rnd branch with the code that read them.

The same day the stack became a list of members, and two files were renamed to say so. The fusion head became the camera head, camera.xgb.json, with the same bytes and hash. The combiner was rewritten in version 1 keyed by member, the same numbers digit for digit and new bytes. The result the detector writes from these files, schema v1, is chapter 3's subject.

For engineers: where each piece lives
  • veilgarde bundle show lists a bundle's artifacts and expected weights, bundle verify re-hashes them, and bundle diff names the roles whose bytes differ between two bundles.
  • A candidate under <work area>/candidates/<id> is a whole bundle of the same shape; veilgarde route <image> --bundle <candidate> runs it.
  • WeightLocator finds the weights by the hash the manifest expects; veilgarde weights list shows where each was found.
