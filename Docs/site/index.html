VeilGarde: an AI-image detector as one Swift package

On what this run built, in the order the code runs, and what was checked.

VeilGarde is a detector for AI-generated images and a crop-robust watermark, in one Swift and MLX package, in two lanes. The detector is a stack of independent members, each with its own score: the camera head over what an image depicts and how it was captured, the screenshot head, the capture signature, the specialist heads and the semantic tiles. Three revisions read them, and one verdict comes out. The watermark reads the warp residual off an image's own pixels and matches a crop or a re-encode against it.

Nine trails walk the code in the order it runs. Two train the camera head and the specialist stack. Five follow detection stage by stage, each handing off to the next: Decode, Shared Signals, Stack, Revisions and Result. Two follow the watermark, signing an image and matching a crop of it.

The ML explorer tells the same flows as machine learning: what each model learns from and why its algorithm was chosen. It also follows how the stack's members and three revisions reach one verdict. Its steps link back to these trails. The research that led here, the warp model, the noiseprint and the studies, is in the record and on the 2026-rnd branch.
